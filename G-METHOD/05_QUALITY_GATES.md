# G Method — Quality Gates

## Gate 0 — Provenance
- [ ] Source filename/version/date recorded.
- [ ] Source-derived statements distinguished from proposals.
- [ ] No invented canonical registry names or document inventory.

## Gate 1 — Architecture integrity
- [ ] IDs are stable and unique.
- [ ] Relationships use registry IDs rather than free-text variants where possible.
- [ ] Applicability rules are explicit.
- [ ] Dependencies and ownership are recorded.

## Gate 2 — Evidence and risk
- [ ] Each maturity assertion has evidence or is marked unverified.
- [ ] Evidence has a source, date, and reviewer.
- [ ] Risks link to owners and controls.
- [ ] KPI definitions include baseline/target/frequency or a documented reason they are absent.

## Gate 3 — Document quality
- [ ] Purpose, scope, audience, owner, status, version, and review date included.
- [ ] Terms match the canonical glossary.
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable, prioritized, and assigned.
- [ ] Confidentiality classification and handling instructions included.

## Gate 4 — Release
- [ ] Required reviewer sign-off obtained.
- [ ] Open discrepancies are visible.
- [ ] No claim of standards certification or scientific validation without evidence.
- [ ] Change log and rollback path recorded.
